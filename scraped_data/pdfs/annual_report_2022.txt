Pakistan
The Pak-Austria Fachhochschule: Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology
(PAF-IAST), established in the public sector by the Government of Khyber
Pakhtunkhwa through an act promulgated in 2019 (Act No. XIV published in
the Gazette of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa on 1st April 2019). The students’ active
enrollment started in October 2020.
The institute is established with the unique vision of skilling Pakistan.  In line with
the needs of the industry, the Institute has started skill-based programs where
the major focus is on the practical implication of education. Skilling Pakistan is
an innovative initiative in the education sector of Pakistan.
The ideal location of PAF-IAST, in the midst of exquisite nature and geographically
perfect surrounding cities including the capital city Islamabad, the divisional
headquarter Abbottabad and the first Digital City of Pakistan, and the Industrial
Zone of Hattar paves the way towards achieving its goal of preparing a workforce
for the industry.
Conforming with the needs of national and international industry, PAF-IAST offers
undergraduate and graduate degree programs in partnership with fourteen (14)
highly reputed universities in Austria and China. Moreover, playing an important
role in revolutionizing the industrial landscape of Pakistan, the institute has
established industry linkage by signing MoUs and MoAs.
PAF-IAST provides a platform for students and faculty members to create their
own startups bridging the gap between education and industry. Nourishing the
inquisitive minds of the students, the university targets their individual skills
required for the market needs, developing them to their full potential.

I am delighted to see the excellent progress being
made by the Pak Austrian Fachhochschule during the
last one year.  The academic programmes have been
expanded from 20 programmes to 46 programmes
last year. The new departments launched include
English and Modern Languages, Psychology, School
of Design, Art and Architecture Technologies (SDAAT),
School of Business, Entrepreneurship, and Professional
Development and the Department of Materials
Science and Engineering. The admissions have also
progressively increased from 183 in the year 2020 to
701 in the year 2022. A sum of Rs. 44 million is being
given for scholarships.
The institution has 100% PhD level faculty and has
been able to generate 8,139 citations reaching a total
of 28,527 citations for the faculty. The faculty has won
eight projects worth Rs.37 million while another 140
projects have been submitted. The highest priority has
been given to quality and a rigorous quality structure
has been implemented to ensure Internal Quality
Assurance. Fourteen Memoranda of Understanding
have been signed with partner Austrian and
Chinese universities while 31 other  Memoranda of
Understanding were signed with universities, research
institutes, industry and community-based public and
private organizations. There has been a special effort
exerted to develop close interactions with industry.
A number of projects of industrial importance have
been undertaken. 386 students have completed
their internships in 158 leading organisations and
there have been more than 45 industrial visits. More
than 300 students are enrolled in community service
programmes.
Particularly praiseworthy has been the focus on
Entrepreneurship. Already there are  4 in-house
startups, and two agreements have been concluded
with IVolve (agreement to commercialize the HPC) and
Rapid dev (Tenancy agreement) to start operations in
PAF-IAST.
I would like to congratulate Prof. Mohammed Mujahid,
Rector, and Prof. Nasser Ali Khan, Project Director,
as well as the Deans and faculty members for their
excellent efforts to make PAF-IAST as a model national
educational institution in Pakistan. Another similar
university is now under construction in Samrial, Sialkot
with close assistance and guidance of Prof. Mohammed
Mujahid and other colleagues. I hope and pray that
there will be a dozen or more organisations created on
this model of a hybrid university with multiple foreign
partner universities eventually offering their degrees,
that was my brainchild. I wish also like to thank Prof.
Haroon Ahmed (Cambridge UK), Prof. Sohail Naqvi,
Prof. A Min Tjoa and late Prof. Michael B. Rode who
have worked closely with me in the establishment of
this wonderful institution under the able leadership of
Prof. Nasser Ali Khan and Prof. Mohammed Mujahid.
I pray for the continued successes of this institution
which will Inshallah rise to great heights and contribute
to the development of this great nation, Inshallah.

Design Clinic
Design Clinic is a startup providing modern digital
design solutions in market 24/7 by utilizing part time
student resources provided at the campus along with
polishing skills of students. With greater population
of Pakistan falling in the young age of under 40 and
freelancing market requirement worldwide design skill
services are most popular among university students
and fresh graduates. It provides an opportunity to earn
money without disturbing other activities. However,
students normally don’t have a serious professional
environment or platform to show their reliability and
make a long-term career in this field. They need to
compete with large number of freelancers to make
their place. Design Clinic is established to provide such
environment to students of PAF-IAST within the walls
of campus. It is also hiring professionals from market
to help students gain professional level competency
at their own campus. Design Clinic will provide non-
stop online services to its customer at time of their
ease. This will open its door to international market
without barrier of time zones.

supply chain and fair trade between farmers.

Industrial Internships
PAF-IAST focuses on skilled based learning to produce
workforce through constant practice of Hands-on
learning. For that purpose, PAF-IAST has adopted
internship-based model for learning which requires
each student to complete mandatory 500 hours of
industrial internship in bachelor programs. This model
is unique in Pakistan and plays crucial role in shaping
the career of the student. 386 students were placed
for an internship during 2022 in 158 organizations
(including in-house internship opportunities at various
departments and centers of PAF-IAST).  The students
also submit a detailed report on each internship
completed in respective semester, which is evaluated
by the panel of experts through interviews.
The mechanism behind this model is to provide
opportunities for soft and hard skills development,
and exposure to develop professional aptitude and
strengthen key competencies required for a particular
job. The marketable skills which are learned through
internship support help the students to get multiple
opportunities for jobs and projects. It also helps them
to get professional feedback and first-hand knowledge
of the market needs.
The quality of education at PAF-IAST is recognized
by the industry. The endorsement by our industrial
partners proves that we are providing skill-based
education as per the industrial demands.

Ultra-high-speed Campus Core
Network
The campus core network of PAF-IAST is based on
40Gbps (upgradable to 100Gbps) fiber optic backbone.
This makes PAF-IAST the privileged higher education
institution in Pakistan to have its backbone network of
this much capacity for high performance computing
with low energy consumption. The ultra-high-speed
backbone network is so deployed to cater for all
digital/ electronic communication needs of PAF-IAST.
There are Five (05) redundant Distribution Switches
for the aggregation of digital traffic from Fifty-Four
(54) Access Switches which are deployed across
the campus. These Access Switches provide access
to the internet and digital resources to Wifi Access
Points, and also transport surveillance video traffic
to the centralized recorder and storage. The core
network is comprised of redundant Core Switches
of 3.84Tbps throughput and 2800 Mbps forwarding
performance, redundant Application Switches for
connecting Compute Nodes, and redundant Firewalls
and Routers with high throughput capacities.

Progress Achieved from
Foreign Tours
PAF-IAST’s top management visited Austria from 19th
June to 2nd July 2022. The following activities were
conducted during their visit:
A meeting with Ms. Bridgitta Blaha, Ex-Ambassador of
Austria in Pakistan, was scheduled. Rector and Project
Director shared the progress report of PAFIAST with
Ms. Blaha. She was apprised of the future requirements
of students’ financial support when they come to
Austria for one-year studies to be completed at the
respective partner UAS.
Ambassador, Mr. Aftab Khokher, was informed
about additional meetings that were being arranged
by PAFIAST with the officials of the International
Cooperation Higher Education Austria (OEAD) and
a successful Entrepreneur, Mr. Stefan Perkmann of
a company called “WhataVenture”. The PAFIAST
delegation
had
useful
interaction
with
them
and requested them to promote the Institute for
networking among the industries they work with.
Mr. Stefan promised to visit PAFIAST in October
2022 to discuss the possibilities of linkages with our
Innovation Technology Park (ITP). A meeting with
Mr. Johanns Bruner, head of business development
for Asia and Pacific in the Austrian Chamber of
Commerce and Industry, Vienna, along with his 3
associates was scheduled. He shared that Austria
requires skilled labor, especially in IT and health
sectors (Nurses). He showed his willingness to link
PAFIAST with Austrian companies. The delegation
had meetings with different representatives from MCI
Innsbruck. They met Andreas Altmann (Rector MCI),
Alex Dumfort (Program Faculty), Werner Stadylmyer
(Program Head), Alex Dumfort (Program Faculty),
Werner Stadlmayr (HoD) and Vladan Antonovic
(Coordinator Transnational Education and Projects).
They discussed the comprehensive engagement
mechanism between PAF-IAST and MCI. Both agreed
upon giving Chemical Engineering students admission
in the MS degree program and providing a Tuition Fee
waiver to PAFIAST students studying at MCI.
Paf-IAST’s delegation visited FH Joanneum, Graz.
Prof. Dr.  Karl Pfeiffer (Rector FHJ), and Dr. Roswitha
Wiedenhofer-Bornemann (Head of the Division),
agreed upon starting a new dual degree program
especially related to medical sciences – nursing,
primary healthcare, ambulatory care, telemedicine,
cardio technologies, micro-CT, etc. They also showed
their interest in co-funding the research activities
to help faculty in developing a winning research
proposal. To help faculty in developing a winning
research proposal, It was reiterated that up to 5
students from each of the 4 cooperating disciplines
will study in FH Joanneum for either the 4th year of
their bachelor studies or 1-2 years of the MS program
of that discipline. FH Joanneum has agreed to offer
an online German Language course to the students
of PAFIAST. Moreover, the second cohort of PAFIAST
faculty is planned to join FHJ in the summer of 2023.
The delegation also visited Green Tech Valley and
met with Mr. Bernhard Puttinger, CEO of Green
Tech Cluster. It was agreed to develop links between
companies based in the PAFIAST technology park
and Green Tech Cluster.
PAFIAST delegation met with Mr. Jakob Calice,
Managing Director, and Mag. Gerhard Volz, Head
of International Cooperation in Higher Education,
OEAD. OEAD suggested that they can assist in
bringing faculty from Austria to PAFIAST under the
Erasmus+ program. For that purpose, FH Graz and
MCI Innsbruck can approach OEAD with proposals. It
was agreed that the availability of OEAD scholarships
(open to anyone) for students (living expenses for
bachelor students EUR 980 and graduate students
EUR1180) will be ensured at the request of Austrian
partner Fachhochschulen for students of PAFIAST.
A meeting was also conducted with the personnel
from the Austrian Ministry of Education, Science
and Research. Mr. Elmar Pichl (Director General -
Head of Higher Education), Ms. Margit Wogowitsch,
(Directorate General V - Head of Strategy Unit), and
Mr. Stephan de Pasqualin (Department of European
Higher Education Area)
At Software Competence Center Hagenberg (SCCH),
the strengthening of cooperation was discussed by
creating a linkage between PAFIAST-ITP and SCCH
in collaborative commercial research. A start-up by Dr.
Maqbool will be launched at ITP in collaboration with
partners in SCCH.
The delegation also visited Joannes Kepler University
(JKU), Linz, and discussed various points of mutual
interest. Moreover, the inclusion of PAFIAST into
ASEA-UniNet membership was also discussed
for Ph.D. programs, summer schools, and general
networking.

2022 was a busy year. PAF-IAST achieved several
milestones as shared in this annual report. The review
and self-assessment of 2022 allowed us to analyze
our steps toward attaining our vision. Every step and
every decision made during 2022 is aligned with our
long-term strategic plan. Based on the previous years’
progress, we were able to prioritize our initiatives and
identify the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for
2023. Some of the macro goals PAF-IAST has set to
shape 2023 are shared in this chapter.

School of Medicine, Dentistry,
and Allied Sciences
PAF-IAST has taken one more step towards the slogan
of skilling Pakistan. The School of Medicine, Dentistry,
and Allied Sciences has been formally approved by
the Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Practical
steps have been taken to ensure the launch of the
state-of-the-art school by Fall-2023.
•
PC-1 and PC-2 for the Establishment of School
of Medicine, Dentistry and Allied Sciences have
been approved.
•
DHQ Hospital Haripur, Cat-C Hospital Khalabat,
Children and Women Care Hospitals are being
notified as “Teaching Hospitals” for affiliation with
Fachhochschule Medical College. These Hospitals
meet the requirement of 500 bedded hospitals.
•
A letter for inspection has been sent to the
Pakistan Medical Commission (PMC) for the visit
and permission for the grant of admissions.
Department of Pharmaceutical
Sciences
PAF-IAST has recently established the Department of
Pharmaceutical Sciences. On the 5th of January 2023,
the visit of the Pharmacy Council of Pakistan (PCP)
was arranged. PAF-IAST is waiting for the decision
of the PCP and to grant the permission to announce
admission to the Bachelor of Pharmacy (B. Pharm)
program. The program is closely associated with
the medical sciences and PAF-IAST takes pride in
developing a skilled workforce with market-oriented
skills to serve our socio-economic needs.
Operationalization of the Pilot
Plants at Mineral Testing
Laboratory
PAF-IAST has achieved a significant milestone
of collaboration with the Government of Khyber
Pakhtunkhwa for the revival and operationalization
of abandoned Pilot Plants at the Mineral Testing
Laboratory. The Plants were established by the Minerals
Development Department of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
On 25th November 2022, PAF-IAST and the Minerals
Development Department signed an agreement
and formally handed over the pilot plants to PAF-
IAST. PAF-IAST will operationalize and utilize these
plants for mineral processing, upgradation, and use
these plants for capacity building and improvement.
These plants will play a key role and serve as a skill
development center in the field of mineral processing
engineering. Plants can extract and upgrade Basic
chromium sulphate BCS, Antimony, Iron, Gold and
Silver, Chromite, Nepheline Syenite, and Copper
practical. A step towards Fachhochschule concept
and skilling Pakistan.
Collaboration with the Industry
PAF-IAST is working on formalizing the Triple Helix
Model and specifically collaboration with the industry.
In the Fachhochschule concept, the industry plays key
role, therefore, we are aiming to get work closely with
the industry for curriculum design, skill identification,
skill development and creating opportunities for
research and development. These areas help both
industry and academia in the longer run. Following
are some of the initiatives planned for 2023.
•
Strategic Collaboration initiated with Yousef
Dewan Companies (YDC) for the purpose of skill
development, internships, certification, diploma,
and other programs.
•
Industrial Open Day where the industries will be
invited to share the areas of mutual interest with
the faculty of PAF-IAST.
•
Initiatives for engagement with industry for faculty
and student internshipsProjects for the industry to
solve their issues as well as product development
•
Contemporary skill development initiative

Conferences and Symposiums
PAF-IAST is focused on the applied research.
Therefore, we are planning to initiate a series of
research conferences and symposiums to provide
platform for applied research and advancements.
Every department/faculty of PAF-IAST will be
arranging conferences and symposiums in 2023 with
the vision of empowering industry through research
and collaboration. Skilling Pakistan with our Short-
Term Education Programs. PAF-IAST is working with
the industry to identify the skill shortage in the industry
and to address the skill shortage issue, PAF-IAST aims
to launch Short Term Education Program (STEP) that
will overcome the skill shortage problem faced by the
industry. Moreover, PAF-IAST has been working with
HEC to provide its services to the Kingdom of Saudi
Arabia’s vision 2030. A proposal has been shared
with the authorities regarding medium and long-term
skill development programs and the establishment of
multifaceted skill entities in Pakistan.
Promoting Entrepreneurial
Mindset
It is mandatory for all undergraduate students enrolled
at PAF-IAST to take entrepreneurship subjects in their
first year of education. Moreover, we are pleased to
share that the Business Incubation Center (BIC) of
PAF-IAST has been approved by HEC and has been
awarded the initial grant for the establishment of BIC.
This platform will create opportunities for our students,
faculty, and alumni to create their own startups.
At PAF-IAST, we are already hosting four in-house
startups. Faculty and students are always encouraged
to start their own businesses and contribute to the
socio-economic development of Pakistan.
Centers for Excellence
PAF-IAST has the most advanced and equipped centers
of excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Railways
and Transportation Systems Engineering. We are in
the process of establishing state of the art centers for
excellence in Agriculture Food Technologies (including
Food Processing and Packaging Technology), Mineral
Resource and Processing, Stem Cell Research Center,
and other centers for excellence. These centers will
be established as part of the triple helix model for
innovation.
