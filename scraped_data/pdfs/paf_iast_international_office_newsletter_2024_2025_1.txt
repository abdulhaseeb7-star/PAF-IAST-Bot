Overall Internationalization funds
The bar chart illustrates a substantial rise in
internationalization funds, increasing from 10.58

Internationalization funds from Austria
The comparative bar chart illustrates the sharp
increase in education-related costs between 2023
and 2024, with tuition fees rising from 6.42 million
PKR to 12.61 million PKR, more than doubling.
Similarly, the monthly stipend surged from 1.05
million PKR to 5.35 million PKR, while airfare costs
escalated from 0.92 million PKR to 4.28 million PKR.
This significant rise suggests increased educational
demand, inflationary pressures, or changes in
funding policies.
Internationalization funds from
PAF-IAST and Chinese Partner

educational expenses, showing that tuition fees
amount to 13.2 million PKR, while accommodation

expenditure to 14.81 million PKR. This visualization
effectively highlights the proportion of costs,
helping stakeholders assess financial distribution.
The clear segmentation aids in better budget
planning and decision-making regarding tuition and
living expenses.
Connecting Innovation to Market: A
Transformative Visit by Mr. Friedrich Christian
Böhmler
During their visit to Beijing Jiaotong University, students explored advancements in rail
technology and big data for traffic management. Hosted by Dr. Long Pan at the Key
Laboratory, they examined innovations in high-speed rail, intelligent signaling, and
automated train operations.
The session also covered big data applications, including real-time analytics, AI-driven
traffic models, and IoT-based monitoring, enhancing traffic flow efficiency and urban
mobility. Additionally, Dr. Lu Xiaohua discussed psychological resilience in transportation,
highlighting stress management for railway operators.
The visit concluded with a Q&A session, fostering collaborative research discussions and
academic exchange opportunities, offering students practical insights into modern
transportation systems.

PAF-IAST at Pakistan Venture Connect 2024
PAF-IAST, led by Rector Prof. Dr. Mohammad Mujahid, participated in Pakistan Venture
Connect 2024, hosted by the Asian Development Bank and CAREC Program at Serena Hotel,
Islamabad. Engaging with leaders from Azerbaijan, Georgia, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, and
Pakistan, the event emphasized regional cooperation and innovation. PAF-IAST leveraged this
platform to foster strategic partnerships, reaffirming its commitment to innovation,
entrepreneurship, and collaboration.
PAF-IAST Startups Gain International Spotlight at
Prime Minister’s Office with Patricia Scotland
PAF-IAST startups Edversity, led by Saad Siddiqui, and EcoZindagi, founded by Areeba Syed,
showcased their innovative ventures at the Prime Minister’s Office, Islamabad, during
Commonwealth Secretary-General Patricia Scotland's inaugural visit to Pakistan. Receiving the
prestigious Excellence Award from Secretary-General Scotland and Chairman of the Prime
Minister’s Youth Programme Rana Mashhood Ahmad Khan, the startups highlighted the global
potential of Pakistani innovation. This recognition reflects PAF-IAST’s dedication to fostering
entrepreneurship with international impact, positioning Pakistan as a rising hub for global
innovation.

Dr. Rizwan Ahmad’s Visit to MCI,
Innsbruck, Austria
During his visit to MCI in Innsbruck, Austria, from January 18 to 24, 2024, Dr. Rizwan
Ahmad held collaborative meetings and lectures to strengthen academic ties and foster research
partnerships. He showcased PAF-IAST’s facilities, generating interest in exchange

research.

Robotics Week 2024 Participation
Dr. Zahid Ullah at THWS, Germany
Dr. Zahid Ullah participated in Robotics Week 2024 at THWS, Germany, representing PAF-

"Dr. Humaira Ayub Attends Genetics Festival
Kickoff with Tribute at Anıtkabir in Ankara"
The 'Genetics Festival' was held in Ankara, hosted by a Genetic Rare Diseases Diagnostic and
Research Center, with 45 scientists from 10 countries participating. The delegation visited Anıtkabir
before engaging in symposiums and training programs. Prof. Dr. Serdar Ceylaner highlighted
Turkey's advanced status in rare diseases research, emphasizing its global collaborations, diagnostic
capabilities, and successful treatments. He stressed the need for investment in personalized medicine
and youth-driven drug production to reduce dependency on costly imported treatments and further
strengthen Turkey’s role as a global leader in genetic research.
