General Instructions/Conditions
2. Following terms and conditions will be applicable in addition to eligibility criteria:
3. Maximum Age limit for Professor is 55, Associate Professor 50, Assistant Professor 40,
Lecturer/Demonstrator is 35 and supporting staff is 45.
4. All the above faculty positions will be filled on a Teaching Track basis and non-faculty on 1 year
contract.
5. Selected candidates shall be governed by the Statutes and Regulations of the Institute.
6. The candidates serving in Government, Semi Government and Autonomous Bodies should route their
applications through proper channel.
7. A person who is dismissed from service or convicted through any Court of Law is not eligible to apply for
the above-mentioned positions.
8. Any applicant who desires to apply for more than one position shall apply separately for each position.
9. Applicants should apply Online on the prescribed application form, available at https://career.paf-
iast.edu.pk/web/login.  They will attach all relevant documents with the online application form before 27-
01-2025.
10. Please do not forget to attach a copy of fee challan (on online application portal) after submission of
application via online portal.
11. Please submit the application fee via digital banking and attach receipt on online portal. fee submitted via
bank draft will not be considered.
12. No application shall be accepted if the copy of deposit receipt is not found attached or the same is not verified
from the bank.
13. Only online submitted applications will be considered.
14. Incomplete applications (online) and applications received in (hard copy) will not be considered.
15. Only shortlisted candidates will be called for test/interviews.
16. No TA/DA will be paid during the selection process.
17. MDC, PAF-IAST reserves the right to increase or decrease the number of positions or reject any or all
applications without assigning any reason.
