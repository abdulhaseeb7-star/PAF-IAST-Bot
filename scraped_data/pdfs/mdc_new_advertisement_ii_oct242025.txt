General Instructions/Conditions
Following terms and conditions will be applicable in addition to eligibility criteria:
• Valid PM&DC registration and PM&DC Eligibility Certificate are mandatory.
• Bring original documents at the time of interview.
• PAF-IAST provides pick-up and drop-off services to its faculty and staff.
• Assistance in locating suitable accommodation in close vicinity of the college will be provided to
selected candidates along with the aforementioned travelling facility.
•
All the above positions will be filled on a contact basis.
•
Selected candidates shall be governed by the Statutes and Regulations of the Institute.
•
The candidates serving in Government, Semi Government and Autonomous Bodies should route their
applications through proper channel.
•
A person who is dismissed from service or convicted through any Court of Law is not eligible to apply
for above mentioned positions.
•
Any applicant who desires to apply for more than one position shall apply separately for each position.
•
Applicants should apply Online on the prescribed application form, available at https://career.paf-
iast.edu.pk/web/login. They will attach all relevant documents with the online application form before
10/11/2025.
•
Please do not forget to attach a copy of fee challan (on online application portal) after submission of
application via online portal.
•
Please submit the application fee via digital banking and attach receipt on online portal. fee submitted
via bank draft will not be considered.
•
No application shall be accepted if the copy of deposit receipt is not found attached or the same is not
verified from the bank.
•
Only online submitted applications will be considered.
•
Incomplete applications (online) and applications received in (hard copy) will not be considered.
•
Only shortlisted candidates will be called for test//interviews.
•
No TA/DA will be paid during the selection process.
•
Age limit for all positions will be determined as per PMDC criteria.
•
SMDAS, PAF-IAST reserves the right to increase or decrease the number of positions or reject any or
all applications without assigning any reason.
