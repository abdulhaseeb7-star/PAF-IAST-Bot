Discipline
Political activities are strictly forbidden at PAF-IAST premises, and all students are
required to give a written undertaking at the time of admission that they will refrain from
politics during their stay at PAF-IAST. The Competent Authority of PAF-IAST has the
audacity to terminate the registration of a student found involved in political activities.
The following practices are strictly prohibited for PAF-IAST students:
-
Theft or damage of the moveable / immovable property in the premises of PAF-IAST.
-
Any misconduct that results in conviction for a crime in a court of competent
jurisdiction may subject a student to disciplinary action including suspension or
expulsion.
-
Possession of firearms, explosives, dangerous chemicals or other weapons on
Campus, or the display or use of the above to intimidate, frighten or control
individuals.
-
Misconduct which obstructs or disrupts teaching, research, administration,
disciplinary procedures, or other activities of the institute.
-
Keeping pets/animals, media players etc or possessing any morally/religiously
objectionable material.
-
Using/distributing/trading drugs, alcohol, narcotic materials and smoking on the
Campus.
