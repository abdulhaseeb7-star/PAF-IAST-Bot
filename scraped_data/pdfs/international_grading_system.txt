4. Grading system
PAF-IAST follows the Relative Grading System in its scheme of studies. Two types of averages are
computed for the grades obtained by a student: The semester grade point average (SGPA) and cumulative
grade point average (CGPA). SGPA indicates the GPA of a course completed in a semester; whereas CGPA
reflects the GPA of all courses completed by a certain date by a student.
