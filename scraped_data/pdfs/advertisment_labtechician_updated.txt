Pak-Austria Fachhochschule
Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology (PAF-IAST)
(A Public Sector Degree Awarding Institute)
PAF-IAST invites applications from qualified and experienced professionals for the following
position:
Position: Molecular Biology Laboratory Technologist / Molecular Biologist / Biotechnologist
Qualification:- MS Molecular Biology Laboratory Technologist, Biotechnology
Experience
The ideal candidate should possess a degree in Molecular Biology, Biotechnology, Medical
Laboratory Technology, Biotechnology or a closely related discipline from an HEC-recognized
institution. Preference will be given to candidates having practical experience in molecular
diagnostics and advanced laboratory techniques.
Required Technical Expertise
The selected candidate should have hands-on experience and demonstrated proficiency in the
following areas:
•
Conventional Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)
•
Real-Time Polymerase Chain Reaction (qPCR)
•
Sanger Sequencing
•
Sequencing workflows and molecular diagnostic technologies
•
Nucleic acid extraction (DNA/RNA) and sample preparation
•
Molecular biology laboratory operations
•
Molecular diagnostic testing procedures
•
Quality assurance and laboratory safety protocols
•
Data analysis, interpretation, and documentation of molecular test results
Key Responsibilities
•
Perform routine and specialized molecular diagnostic tests.
•
Conduct PCR, qPCR, and sequencing-based analyses.
•
Extract and prepare nucleic acid samples for downstream applications.
•
Maintain laboratory equipment and ensure compliance with quality standards.
•
Record, analyze, and report laboratory findings accurately.
•
Assist in the development and optimization of molecular diagnostic workflows.
•
Ensure adherence to biosafety and laboratory quality management procedures.
