Faculty of Management and Social Sciences
The Faculty of Management and Social Sciences at Pak-Austria Fachhochschule: Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology is a prestigious academic department dedicated to empowering students through skilling in its diverse degree programs. Comprising the School of Business, Entrepreneurship, and Professional Development (SBEPD), along with the Department of English and Modern Languages, the faculty offers a comprehensive education that combines theoretical knowledge with practical skills. The faculty’s renowned professors, with their wealth of expertise, foster an intellectually stimulating learning environment, encouraging students to think critically, develop innovative problem-solving abilities, and cultivate a global perspective. By emphasizing skilling as a core aspect of their degree programs, the faculty equips students with the necessary tools and competencies to excel in their chosen fields, become successful entrepreneurs, and contribute positively to society and the economy.
