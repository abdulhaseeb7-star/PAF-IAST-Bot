Cancellation of Admission
Admission is liable to be cancelled if the candidate is found guilty of suppression or misrepresentation of material facts at any stage.
The seats of those candidates who, after submission of admission dues, do not join within 15 days of commencement date of programs will be declared vacant and filled up by candidates next on merit.
