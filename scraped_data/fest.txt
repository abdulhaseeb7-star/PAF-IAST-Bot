Faculty of Engineering, Sciences, & Technology (FEST)
Welcome to the Faculty of Engineering, Sciences, & Technology (FEST), a dynamic and innovative faculty dedicated to advancing technological and scientific boundaries through comprehensive education and cutting-edge research. FEST is home to a broad spectrum of engineering disciplines, each housed within specialized departments designed to foster expertise and drive interdisciplinary collaboration. Our departments include Electrical & Computer Engineering, Chemical and Energy Engineering, Mechanical & Manufacturing Engineering, Materials Science & Engineering, Mineral Resource Engineering, Civil Engineering and Town Planning, Transportation Engineering, and Biomedical Engineering.
At FEST, our mission is to equip students with the critical skills and knowledge needed to address the challenges of the modern world. Through a blend of rigorous coursework, practical laboratory experiences, and real-world problem-solving projects, we prepare our graduates to become leaders in their respective fields. Our faculty is composed of renowned scholars and industry professionals who are deeply committed to mentoring the next generation of engineers and technologists. Whether it’s developing sustainable energy solutions, advancing healthcare technologies, or designing smart cities, FEST is at the forefront of engineering innovation.
