Finance Directorate
About Department
The Directorate of Finance started functioning as Finance Section immediately after the establishment of the university in 2015.
Presently, the affairs of Directorate of Finance are run by One Director along-with his support staff. The directorate facilitates students and PAF-IAST staff, both Faculty & Administrative about visiting faculty payments, Board of Studies, Study Tours, Sports Activities, etc. Besides, students are facilitated and guided in fee related matters, fee refunds and fee clearance matters.
Vision & Mission
Vision
:
The Finance Department aspires to deliver effective and efficient services to PAF-IAST employees and other donor agencies keeping in view the fiscal integrity.
Mission
:
The mission of the Finance Department is to deliver timely and accurate financial reporting and provide proper oversight of all expenditures and revenue collections to successfully support the operations of the Institute.
