Faculty of Life Sciences (FLS)
Welcome to the
Faculty of Life Sciences (FLS)
, a cornerstone of excellence in health education and research.
FLS
is dedicated to preparing students for vital roles in healthcare through a deeply interdisciplinary approach that spans several key areas of medical and health sciences. Our faculty comprises the Department of
Biological and Health Sciences
(BHS), Department of
Pharmaceutical Sciences
, the
School of Medicine
, and the Department of
Dentistry and Allied Health Sciences
. Each department is committed to delivering top-tier education, fostering clinical competencies, and advancing healthcare through innovative research.
At
FLS
, we pride ourselves on creating a learning environment that combines rigorous academic coursework with practical, hands-on experiences in clinical and laboratory settings. Our programs are designed to equip students with the necessary skills to excel in their careers while addressing the complex health challenges of today’s world. From pioneering pharmaceutical developments to training skilled clinicians and healthcare providers,
FLS
is at the forefront of enhancing health and well-being across communities.
